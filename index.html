How to get started with JazzCash

This independent guide helps you find official account registration instructions. Registration options and requirements may change, so confirm the latest steps directly with JazzCash.

Keep your account safe: This page does not create accounts and will never ask for your phone number, CNIC, PIN, password, or OTP. Never share your PIN or verification code with anyone.

Use official channels

  1. Visit the official JazzCash website using the button below, or find the official JazzCash app through your device’s trusted app store.
  2. Follow the account registration instructions shown in the official app or published by JazzCash.
  3. If you need help, contact JazzCash using support details listed on its official website.

Security reminders

  • Check the website address before entering any information.
  • Do not open unexpected links promising instant account activation.
